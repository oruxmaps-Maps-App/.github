**So, what is oruxmaps, exactly?**
oruxmaps is a GPS route-tracking and offline mapping app for Windows that helps outdoor users plan a trip, follow it, and log where they went. It's aimed squarely at people whose route planning starts online but ends up somewhere without a signal.

<img src="https://www.oruxmaps.com/cs/images/headers/header3.png" alt="oruxmaps Screenshot"/>
*A saved route shown inside oruxmaps alongside a downloaded offline map.*

### Is oruxmaps right for you?
- `[Offline]` Keeps map areas usable with no connection once they're downloaded
- `[Tracking]` Records distance, elevation, and time as you move
- `[Sources]` Supports a range of online and offline map sources rather than one fixed provider
- `[Waypoints]` Lets you mark and label stops or landmarks along a route

**What's next:** future updates are expected to keep refining map source options and track handling, though no specific timeline is set.
